Triple Diamond Realty — California Compliance Package (Final, Publish-Ready)
Below is the complete legal framework for a California-only investor-focused brokerage website, built on the strongest patterns from New Western's terms and tightened to California-specific regulations: California DRE (Bus. & Prof. Code §§10140–10148, Reg. 2773), California Civil Code (TDS/Natural Hazard), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), California Fair Employment & Housing Act (FEHA), California-amended TCPA rules, California ADA case law (Unruh Act exposure), and California Civil Code §1632 (language-of-negotiation rules).

CALIFORNIA REGULATORY MAP (What Actually Applies to You)
DRE Advertising Rules — Bus. & Prof. Code §10140.6 + DRE Reg. 2773: Every solicitation (website, ad, business card, email, SMS, video, social post) must include the responsible broker's identity and DRE license number, plus any agent's DRE number if featured. Must appear on the first page or screen the consumer sees — not just buried in a sub-page footer.
Team Name Rules — Bus. & Prof. Code §10159.6: If you use any DBA or team name ("Triple Diamond Deals," etc.), it must be registered with the DRE.
California Civil Code §2079 / §1102: When representing buyers or sellers of 1–4 unit residential properties, you owe duties of inspection and disclosure (TDS, Natural Hazard Disclosure, Megan's Law database notice, lead-based paint, etc.).
California Fair Housing — FEHA (Gov. Code §12955): Broader than federal law. Protected classes include: race, color, religion, sex, gender, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, source of income (including Section 8), disability, veteran/military status, genetic information, age, citizenship, primary language, and immigration status.
CCPA / CPRA (Civil Code §1798.100 et seq.): Applies to your business if you have $25M+ revenue, OR buy/sell/share personal info of 100,000+ California residents per year, OR derive 50%+ revenue from selling personal info. Most small brokerages don't hit the threshold — but compliance is still best practice and DRE has signaled it expects it. Build it in now.
California TCPA Layer: California's mini-TCPA tracks federal but state courts are aggressive. Always get prior express written consent before any auto-dialed call or marketing SMS.
California Civil Code §1632 — Language of Negotiation: If you negotiate primarily in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, or Korean, you must provide a translated version of certain contracts. Add a notice on your site.
Unruh Civil Rights Act (Civil Code §51) + California ADA: Websites are routinely targeted. Comply with WCAG 2.1 AA.
NAR Clear Cooperation Policy: If you publicly market a listing (including on your site or social media), it must be in the MLS within one business day. Critical for your off-market variant page.
RESPA Section 8: If TDR receives compensation for referring to a title, escrow, lender, or affiliated provider, disclose via an Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure.
